Why VendorLink

South Korea Needs a Unified
Wearable Payment Standard

Despite being a global technology leader, South Korea lacks a unified wearable payment method. We're building the software infrastructure to change that — enabling seamless QR-based transactions from any smartwatch, with or without internet connectivity.

The Problem

  • ✕ No unified wearable payment standard exists in South Korea
  • ✕ Existing solutions are fragmented across proprietary ecosystems
  • ✕ Offline payment capability remains largely unsupported
  • ✕ Hardware vendors lack interoperable payment software
  • ✕ QR-code based wearable payments are virtually non-existent

Our Solution

  • ✓ Unified payment software platform for all smartwatch hardware
  • ✓ QR code based payments — scan and transact instantly
  • ✓ Works with or without internet connectivity (offline mode)
  • ✓ B2B software licensing — companies integrate on their hardware
  • ✓ Multi-VPA support with real-time transaction notifications
Our Technology

Enterprise-Grade Payment
Software Platform

Our payment infrastructure is designed from the ground up for reliability, security, and interoperability. Companies license our software to deploy on their wearable hardware.

01

QR-Based Payment Engine

Dynamic QR code generation and scanning optimized for small wearable screens. Supports both merchant-presented and customer-presented QR flows.

02

Offline Transaction Protocol

Proprietary offline transaction storage and synchronization. Payments are securely cached and reconciled when connectivity is restored via MQTT protocol.

03

Multi-VPA Architecture

Support for multiple Virtual Payment Addresses on a single device. Vendors and businesses can manage separate payment channels seamlessly.

04

Hardware-Agnostic SDK

Our software integrates with any smartwatch platform. Companies license the SDK to embed payment capabilities directly into their existing wearable products.

05

Real-Time Notifications

Instant haptic and visual payment confirmations. Transaction status, balance updates, and security alerts delivered in real-time to the wrist.

06

Bank-Grade Security

End-to-end encryption, two-factor authentication, and secure element integration. Built to meet the most stringent financial regulatory requirements.
